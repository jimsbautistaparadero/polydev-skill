# Thesis / Capstone Chapter Outline

> Adapt to the institution's required format before drafting.

## Front matter

- Title page
- Approval/endorsement page
- Originality/declaration statement, if required
- Acknowledgments, if required
- Abstract
- Table of contents
- Lists of tables, figures, abbreviations

## Chapter 1 — Introduction

1. Background and context
2. Evidence of the practical problem
3. Research gap and rationale
4. Statement of the problem / research questions
5. General and specific objectives
6. Significance/contribution
7. Scope, delimitations, and limitations
8. Definition of key terms

## Chapter 2 — Review of Related Literature and Studies

1. Conceptual/theoretical foundations
2. Theme 1: [topic]
3. Theme 2: [topic]
4. Theme 3: [topic]
5. Related empirical studies and comparable systems
6. Methodological synthesis and limitations
7. Synthesis and research gap
8. Framework, if required in this chapter

## Chapter 3 — Methodology

1. Research design and rationale
2. Study setting/context
3. Population, sampling, and participants/data
4. Instruments and validation/pilot
5. Data collection / system development procedure
6. Evaluation/testing procedure
7. Data analysis
8. Ethical considerations

## Chapter 4 — Results and Discussion (only after actual evidence exists)

Organize sections by research question/objective. For each:

- Evidence and analysis method
- Result with appropriate table/figure
- Interpretation
- Comparison with literature
- Limitations/alternative explanations

## Chapter 5 — Summary, Conclusions, and Recommendations

1. Summary of the study
2. Summary of findings by research question
3. Conclusions bounded by the evidence
4. Recommendations grounded in findings
5. Future research/development

## References

Use the required citation style and verify each entry.

## Appendices

- Approved instruments
- Consent and information sheets
- Permission letters/approvals
- Detailed test cases and evidence
- Supplementary outputs
- Other institution-required materials
